Four ways to build capability

Training starts with the person, the equipment and the process being used.

  • Human spray training — setup, pressure, nozzle selection, basecoat, clearcoat, spot repair and disciplined technique
  • Apprentice development — practical learning matched to experience and supervision
  • Process training — materials, paintshop flow, booth utilisation and quality
  • Product-change training — support a move to a different refinish system, tool or working method
02
