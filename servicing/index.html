01

Know what you have. Know what is due.

An equipment record is a practical place to start a preventative-maintenance conversation.

  • Make, model, serial number and location
  • Last inspection or service date
  • Known faults and operating symptoms
  • Operator training and routine checks
02

Agree the support before it is needed

Sagola equipment service enquiries need model-specific scope, authorised procedures and clear escalation.

  • Planned inspection and service requirements
  • Parts and consumable availability
  • Factory escalation and warranty responsibilities
  • Service history and refresher-training needs
03

Keep a usable equipment history

A service record should follow the equipment, not disappear into an email chain.

  • Asset make, model, serial number and location
  • Inspection dates, completed work, faults and parts used
  • Annual and preventative maintenance requirements
  • Operator refreshers, PPE review and approved checks
  • Next service date and named responsible person
  • Future reminders depend on an agreed system connection
Scope & availability

Service packages, turnaround, response hours, warranty and parts commitments are quoted only once agreed with the relevant provider. Automated service reminders are a future integration.
