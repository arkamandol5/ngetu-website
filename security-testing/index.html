A useful review makes the next decision clearer.

Security assessments differ in scope and depth. Start with the business question and the environment, then agree the permitted work in writing with the system owner.

01

Define the boundary

A request for information does not authorise testing. Scope should state the systems, methods, timing, exclusions and escalation arrangements.

  • Identify the accountable system owner
  • Choose the assessment appropriate to the question
  • Keep credentials and sensitive details out of public forms
02

Turn findings into action

Agree how results will be prioritised, who will receive them and what follow-up is included.

  • A clear explanation of business impact
  • Practical remediation priorities
  • Retesting within a separately agreed scope
