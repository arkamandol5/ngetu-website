02

Three different kinds of approval

Use the exact claim for the exact programme, product, territory and period.

  • Repairer or manufacturer programmes relate to specified repair operations and requirements
  • Paint-system approvals relate to the approved products or systems
  • Equipment homologations relate to their stated application or compatibility
  • A product deployment is not automatically manufacturer approval of the supplier
03

  • Logo permission and brand clear space
  • Review date and withdrawal or update process
