Essential site behavior

The hosting platform may use authentication cookies when you sign in. The site uses browser storage for your measurement choice and temporary campaign labels.

  • Measurement preference: local storage until changed or cleared
  • Campaign labels: session storage for the current tab
  • Sagola quote basket: item identifiers, quantities and configuration notes in local storage until you remove them or clear browser data. Keep personal or confidential details out of configuration notes. Contact fields are not saved to the basket.
  • Checkout session: a private order-access token, request identifier and order identifier are kept in session storage for this tab. Contact and delivery form fields are not saved there. Do not share your private order link.
  • No personal form values are stored in campaign metadata
02

Optional measurement

Only after you allow measurement, the site can record anonymous counts for supported page, call, WhatsApp, enquiry, demo and download events.

  • No name, phone, email, message or complete query string is included
  • You can change your choice through Measurement preferences in the footer
  • Rejecting optional measurement does not prevent browsing or enquiry preparation
