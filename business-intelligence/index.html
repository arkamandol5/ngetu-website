01

Nine questions worth answering

A dashboard earns its place when somebody can make a better decision from it.

  • Which jobs and services contribute margin?
  • Where is workshop capacity being lost?
  • Which costs are changing, and why?
  • How consistent is performance between sites?
  • Where are invoice and approval delays?
  • Which customers or work types need attention?
  • What is the relationship between materials and rework?
  • Which equipment has maintenance due?
  • What changed after the last improvement?
02

Build a trustworthy view

The project depends on authorised data access, consistent definitions and clear ownership.

  • Accounting, CRM, ERP and workshop data discovery
  • Data quality and common performance definitions
  • Reporting, dashboards and consolidated analytics
  • Ongoing support and improvement planning
Scope & availability

Every data connection requires technical discovery and permission. Automated equipment reminders are future possibilities, not currently confirmed integrations.
