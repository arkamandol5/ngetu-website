Make the current route visible

Look for duplicated capture, unclear ownership and missing information. Separate routine invoices from exceptions.

  • Record volumes and common exception types
  • Identify approvers and escalation

Validate the proposed route

A demonstration should show a representative invoice, its approval and the supported accounting connection.

  • Confirm regional and system compatibility
  • Agree implementation and support scope
  • Measure status visibility and manual handling after the change
