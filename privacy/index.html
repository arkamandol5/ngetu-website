01

What this review site does

The site operates in review mode until NGETU’s production details and intake are configured. The enquiry screen states whether you are saving a local draft or sending an enquiry.

  • Local draft mode does not send the values you enter
  • When live intake is enabled, required details are stored in the site enquiry system for follow-up
  • No passwords, confidential repair records or sensitive system evidence should be entered
  • Hosting and authentication providers may process technical access records
02

Your choices

You may choose whether to enable optional measurement. A local draft can be downloaded or cleared without sending it.

  • Optional measurement is off until you choose it
  • Campaign labels are retained only for the current browser tab session
  • No personal form values are sent in analytics events
  • Use the verified contact options shown on Contact for a request about your information once configured
03

Online orders and bespoke quotes

When online sales are enabled, contact and delivery details, selected products, accepted terms version and payment references are stored for order processing. The payment provider processes payment details on its own page; this website does not collect card numbers.

  • Business Central receives order or quote details for NGETU to process
  • Authorised staff can access order records and manage fulfilment
  • The final notice must identify the configured payment provider, Microsoft and hosting processors, and define order/accounting retention
  • Order status is available through a private browser token; keep that link private
04

Before customer launch

The final responsible entity, privacy contact, retention period, service-provider details and rights-request process must be confirmed by NGETU.

  • Production intake remains disabled until the required settings are approved
  • The final notice must match the implemented CRM and contact workflow
  • This review notice is not a certification of POPIA compliance
Scope & availability

Preview notice, not a final production privacy policy. Do not enter passwords, identity documents, customer repair files or other sensitive information.
