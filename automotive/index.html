01

Make the handovers work

A connected repair journey starts with clear responsibilities and reliable information.

  • Capture information where the work happens
  • Make the next action and its owner visible
  • Agree which records and permissions each system needs
  • Review delays and rework across the complete job
02

Build the operation around its people

Start with one practical problem, then consider the related processes. Connection is a discovery and implementation task—not a claim that every product already exchanges data.

  • A clear owner for every handover
  • Training matched to the process and equipment
  • A service record for assets that keep the workshop moving
  • A measured next step, with a scope and an accountable partner
