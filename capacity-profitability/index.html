Look beyond a busy workshop

Activity can hide waiting time or repeated work. Follow the handovers and measure a consistent set of jobs.

  • Estimate versus actual labour and materials
  • Work waiting for parts, approval or preparation
  • Quality and completion patterns
02

Choose one measurable improvement

Use operational records to decide what to change, then compare like-for-like work.

  • Name a process owner
  • Set a baseline and review period
  • Check the effect on both throughput and contribution
