Insights / Equipment

Good application begins with cared-for equipment.

Use the manufacturer’s current instructions for the exact equipment. A web article cannot prescribe a service interval for every gun, coating and working environment.

01

Build an equipment record

Identify the make, model, serial number, use and last service. An accurate record makes a technical conversation much more useful.

  • Record changes in pattern, finish or air/material behavior
  • Document cleaning and maintenance already performed
  • Avoid unapproved modifications or improvised repairs
02

Get the scope right

Inspection, cleaning, consumables and repair are different tasks. Agree the inspection and authorised procedure before work starts.

  • Confirm parts and compatible consumables
  • Keep a record of completed work
  • Agree the next inspection and operator refresher
