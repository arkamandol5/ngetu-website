01

Build the movement before using paint

SagolaSPRAY provides repeatable virtual practice and feedback on core spraying technique.

  • Gun-to-surface distance
  • Hand angle and fan orientation
  • Movement speed and consistency
  • Repeated exercises and instructor feedback
02

Fit the programme around the learner

Discuss a demonstration and the teaching environment before deciding on a programme.

  • Colleges and apprenticeship providers
  • Repair-group and dealership training centres
  • Experienced painters refreshing core technique
  • Instructors planning guided practice and assessment
03

Keep virtual learning connected to real work

VR supplements practical learning. It does not replace supervised booth work, coating-safety training, respiratory protection or an assessed qualification.

  • Confirm current software, hardware and licence requirements
  • Agree instructor orientation and learner outcomes
  • Plan the transition to supervised practical work
  • Confirm local availability, programme rights and fees
Scope & availability

Demonstration availability, training rights, licensing and any accreditation are confirmed before booking. No qualification or exclusive platform claim is implied.
