01

4240 air-fed visor

The manufacturer describes a compressed-air-fed face shield with a belt-mounted regulator and audible low-flow warning. It requires suitable breathing-quality air and the complete approved configuration.

  • Read the current manual for the exact model
  • Confirm applicable certification and local requirements with a competent adviser
  • Plan user training, checks, maintenance and replacement items
02

Choose protection for the actual task

Filtering masks, air-fed equipment and paint-room coveralls serve different purposes. They are not interchangeable.

  • Filtering respirators are not suitable for spraying isocyanate paints
  • Discuss MP300 and particulate-mask options only for assessed suitable tasks
  • Do not treat paint-room workwear as chemical-protective clothing without the applicable approval
03

Include the air supply and maintenance

Equipment selection must consider the whole working environment.

  • 4240 is not self-contained breathing apparatus or an escape device
  • A regulator filter does not make contaminated compressor air safe to breathe
  • Confirm breathing-air testing, compatible hoses and servicing
  • Agree booth controls, training and the site-specific safe working procedure
Scope & availability

This overview is not a PPE prescription or a safety procedure. Use the exact manufacturer instructions, a competent workplace assessment and applicable South African requirements. UK HSE guidance below explains the isocyanate hazard; it is not a statement of South African law.
